# Cardiac acquisition research — 2026-10-30

## Accepted initial subset

[BodyParts3D 4.0 archive](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html):
same coordinate convention as the body, publisher concept-to-element tables and
exact source components. Reduced surfaces offer real named components, not a
complete photorealistic organ or validated wall mechanics. The 30-part subset is
accepted only as sourced educational geometry pending independent review.

## Candidates, not imported or licensed by this change

- [NIH 3D Heart Library](https://3d.nih.gov/collections/heart-library): image-derived
  heart models useful for education, including congenital disease. An individual
  case's exact files/license, pathology, consent/de-identification and redistribution
  rights must be checked. NIH hosting is not blanket commercial permission, and a
  pathological lumen model is not a normal complete wall/valve atlas. No candidate
  files are accepted or registered into this body.
- Imaging segmentation in 3D Slicer can derive structures supported by a suitable
  licensed acquisition, not recover valves/cordae below image resolution. Exact
  image/data rights, anonymization and independent expert segmentation are needed.
- [3DHeartLab part-file services](https://www.3dheartlab.com/digital-prototyping)
  offer an acquisition conversation for image-derived STL/OBJ parts. Export
  availability/pricing does not establish public browser embedding, raw public
  repository redistribution, complete normal anatomy or medical review coverage.
  Obtain a written, exact-file web-application license and quote first.
- Catfish request remains separate. No proprietary application resources were
  extracted, evaluated or purchased here. An app subscription is not an asset
  redistribution license.

## Acquisition priorities

1. Registered normal four-chamber myocardial walls, septa, endocardial surfaces
   and four-valve annuli/chordae/papillary apparatus, with source imaging/review.
2. Coronary sinus ostium, cardiac-vein tributaries, pulmonary/caval ostia and
   documented coronary-dominance/venous variants, without duplicated segments.
3. Pericardial layers and source-supported conduction structures; only explicitly
   schematic representations if exact gross-anatomy geometry cannot be supported.
4. Licensed tissue textures, separately audited LODs and time-resolved motion.

Ask suppliers for stable anatomy IDs, separate mesh coverage, units/axes,
registration landmarks, independent medical review, conversion/adaptation and
commercial/public-browser delivery rights. No purchase or legal acceptance is
authorized by this report. No budget or clinical-quality guarantee is assumed.
