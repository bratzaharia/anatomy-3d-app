# Internal anatomy and valve limits

Accepted: eleven publisher valve components (three tricuspid, two mitral, three
pulmonary, three aortic), five source papillary components and four cavity
surfaces. They load separately from the default external package and have stable
IDs, picking, opacity, isolation, colors and focus.

Publisher pulmonary/aortic cusp orientation terms are preserved. They have **not**
been automatically relabelled as right-coronary, left-coronary or noncoronary cusps.
FMA identity/source terms and provisional Latin/RO translations remain visible.

There is no accepted complete annular/chordal apparatus, reconstructed myocardial
fiber tract, ostial seal or biomechanical valve motion. Endocardium/epicardium and
pericardial layers are not generated from offset shells. Source cavity boundaries
are not substitute tissue layers.

Clipping is an uncapped geometric section through real source triangles. It is
not a simulated histological wall, CT, MRI, echo view or validated internal-camera
protocol. Cut-away surfaces cannot be raycast-selected where discarded.

Future acquisition needs independently separable leaflets, annuli, chordae,
papillary attachments, root/ostia, registered myocardial walls and expert review
of their relationships. No heartbeat or valve-motion controls are presented as
implemented without those data.
