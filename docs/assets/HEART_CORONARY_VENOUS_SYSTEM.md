# Shared cardiac venous subset

No new venous mesh is imported or classified as cardiac muscle. The cardiac panel
reuses the exact existing IDs for the coronary sinus, great/middle/small cardiac
veins, posterior vein of the left ventricle, left/right marginal veins and anterior
cardiac vein. These remain unreviewed BodyParts3D educational surfaces.

Reference graph: great, middle, small and posterior-left-ventricular veins drain
to the coronary sinus, which drains to the right atrial cavity. Anterior cardiac
veins drain directly to the right atrium; they are not forced through the sinus.
The smallest cardiac veins and oblique left atrial vein remain unavailable; no
fictional tributaries or Thebesian-valve geometry are drawn.

Both venae cavae drain to the right atrial cavity. The four named superior/
inferior left/right pulmonary veins drain to the left atrial cavity. Their type
remains **vein**, irrespective of oxygenated blood. The common arrangement is
not asserted to cover all pulmonary-venous variations.

The interface exposes 17 existing related venous inventory entries (16 available,
one unavailable); these include pulmonary source sets, not 17 independent named
vessels. The venous manifest remains authoritative for its individual/set counts.

Semantic drainage is not a certificate of physical watertight openings. Junction
distances, coronary-sinus ostium, valve orientation, wall completeness and all
source-to-chamber relationships need independent anatomical review. No meshes
are welded, translated or inflated to fabricate continuity.

Reference: [OpenStax 19.1](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/19-1-heart-anatomy).
The existing venous source research and manifest retain their original citations.
