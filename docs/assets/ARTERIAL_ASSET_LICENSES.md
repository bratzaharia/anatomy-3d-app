# Arterial licenses and provenance

Integrated geometry: **BodyParts3D 4.0, 99% reduced IS-A OBJ archive**:
`https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/data/bodyparts3d/LATEST/isa_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip`.
The current [publisher license](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html)
(updated 2025-02-27, checked 2026-11-01) states CC BY 4.0, including redistribution
and adapted data subject to attribution. No new ShareAlike/NC dataset enters the
arterial runtime. Retain all existing source-specific licenses for other layers.

Required attribution embedded in each GLB and shipped as text:

> BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International

Changes: deterministic mm-to-m proper rotation, regenerated normals, merged
source components by canonical ID, lossless Meshopt packaging, original textures
not supplied. No smoothing/remeshing/mirroring/nonuniform body fit is applied.
FJ2013 is recorded as an exact duplicate of FJ1846 and not rendered twice.

Metadata tables, ZIP directory and every selected OBJ have SHA-256 records in
the manifest; the importer pins them on reimport. `sourceAliases` retains the
excluded duplicate evidence. Shared cardiac GLB hashes are unchanged. Visible
source/license links remain in the arterial panel and general model attribution.

No proprietary assets, software-app extraction, new paid licenses, patient CT/
MRI/DICOM, public upload or deployment are included. Supplier promises require
asset-specific written rights before acquisition. Source mapping does not imply
medical certification. FMA publisher IDs are not newly invented FIPAT codes;
official TA2 tables are not copied or machine-translated here.
