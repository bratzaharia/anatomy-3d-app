# Brain medical validation — pending

No anatomical region, color map, translation or registration is clinically
approved by this feature. Technical source identity is not medical certification.
All 53 parts reuse existing unreviewed BodyParts3D/FMA mappings; none are promoted
to verified simply because GLB decoding, tests or screenshots pass.

## Technical evidence

`brain-geometry-audit.json` checks pinned asset bytes, unique nodes, canonical IDs,
exact source counts, decoded topology/position hashes, finite normals/positions,
index bounds, geometric bounds and source-side consistency. It records that no
atlas label attributes, matched template or spherical correspondence is present.
Only those independent surfaces are eligible for region coloring.

Hemisphere controls refer to available lateralized source parts. No new hemisphere,
vermis, nucleus, sulcus, ventricular surface or sectional cap is fabricated.
Deep structures remain in place; isolation hides surrounding geometry rather
than moving tissue. Conventional palette colors do not imply histological tissue
colors, blood flow, neural activation, a patient scan or measured brain function.

## Required human review

1. Verify every source FMA identity, laterality, tissue classification and mapping.
2. Review EN/RO/Latin terminology and clarify partial/cross-lobar presentation groups.
3. Inspect source boundaries, folding, incomplete/overlapping surfaces and location
   relative to fixed skull, brainstem, nerves and vascular anatomy from all views.
4. Confirm that a whole source part is not misrepresented as a finer parcel or a
   full hemisphere; review unavailable targets explicitly.
5. For every future atlas: check exact version/license/template and label IDs,
   demonstrate a valid correspondence chain, quantify independent held-out
   registration quality and review hemisphere/medial-wall/boundary consistency.
6. Record reviewer identity/qualifications/date, exact source and derived hashes,
   accepted/rejected structures, evidence and remaining uncertainty separately.

No MRI reference or cortical spherical topology is currently available for this
BodyParts3D subject. Therefore no detailed atlas transfer is accepted. A standalone
validated atlas surface is an alternative research route, not permission to
silently replace or deform the existing reference brain or skull.
