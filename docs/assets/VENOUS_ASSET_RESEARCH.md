## Recommended sequence

1. Keep the current documented free baseline; finish independent regional review.
2. Request a **gap-focused** free/evaluation sample: intracranial sinuses and
   head/neck drainage, then distal/perforating veins and vertebral/pelvic plexuses.
3. Ask for a compatible body reference, named meshes, sample topology/units,
   counterpart landmarks, normal-vs-variant and sex-specific scope, clinical
   review record, and separate endpoints/tributaries. Measure registration before
   licensing an entire collection. Do not patch connections by invented tubes.
4. Obtain explicit browser-delivered asset, adaptation, mobile distribution,
   attribution, geographic/team and repository/storage permissions. Standard
   asset-store terms can conflict with public GLB delivery.
5. Present exact quote and permitted uses for owner approval. Retain free and
   paid variants as separately audited sources; do not mix overlapping trees.

Independent anatomist/clinician time is a separate budget item; no quoted review
cost is known. Buying a visually realistic pack cannot make the app “100% medical”.
