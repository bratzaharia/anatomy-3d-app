# Cerebral circulation — source geometry, no invented territories

59 source records are available in the cerebral category, including ACA,
source M1/M2 portions, posterior communicating arteries, anterior communicating
artery, basilar artery, source P1/P2 portions, cerebellar vessels and selected
cortical/perforating branches. Internal carotids and vertebrals are in head/neck.
FJ files ending in M are publisher-supplied counterparts; the importer performs
no mirroring, cortex painting or coordinate fitting.

The cerebral category can be filtered, searched, multiselected, recolored,
focused and isolated. The cited M1-to-aorta origin pathway is implemented.
Small vessels also have list-based selection; expanded screen-space or
centerline picking is not claimed. A sparse click grid is not a reliable test
of a thin M1 mesh, so raycast exclusion is tested separately on the aorta.

**BLOCKED/PENDING DATA:** separately segmented ACA A1/A2, additional distal MCA
portions, complete perforator enumeration and source-specific Circle of Willis
variants. The ACA mesh must not be mislabeled as a separately segmented A1.
The available geometry must not imply that every person has a symmetric circle.

ACA/MCA/PCA/cerebellar/brainstem perfusion territories remain unavailable in 3D.
The existing brain has 53 anatomical source parts but no registered vascular
territory atlas. Displaying a painted approximation would be misleading.

Required future gate: dataset-specific redistribution license, reference space,
label table and version, documented surface/volume correspondence, measured
registration error and qualified boundary/laterality review. Failure leaves
territory rendering disabled. No static atlas is presented as patient perfusion.
